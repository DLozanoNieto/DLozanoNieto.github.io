Diego Lozano
Email

Diego is a early career photographer based out of Vancouver, BC who explores urban environments, city life, and abstracts the everyday. His range of work covers expressionist scenes that engage to viewers, the scale of human "progress", and the frivolities found in the world. At it's core, his images hope to invoke a feeling, emotion, memory, or desire; to slow down, observe, question, and appreciate what exists around us.
